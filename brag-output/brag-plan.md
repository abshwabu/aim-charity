# Brag Plan: Aim Charity

## What is this app?
Aim Charity is a coalition platform ("a group of groups") uniting grassroots community organizations across Ethiopia to coordinate relief, eliminate duplicate logistics, and deliver 100% transparent direct mutual aid to families in need.

## The angle
The core visual and philosophical premise is **"Many groups, one circle"**: grassroots groups rooted in Ethiopian woredas and towns joining hands. It highlights the power of local solidarity, direct unbureaucratic delivery, and radical financial transparency where every single birr is publicly audited.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An organic interlocking circle emblem blooms in deep emerald green and warm amber against a warm cream backdrop, revealing the warm Fraunces editorial headline: *"When Communities Unite, Hope Becomes Real"*, followed by three floating metric chips popping into place (`6 Coalitions`, `142K+ People Helped`, `100% Direct Aid`).

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Coalition Mosaic**: Four distinct member group cards arrive sequentially, showcasing community elders, mutual aid associations, youth technicians, and women cooperatives working together without silos.
- **Direct Programs in Action**: Food reserves, solar-powered boreholes, and mobile health clinics shown with concrete real-world impact numbers and verified registry seals.
- **The Radical Transparency Counter**: High-contrast animated counter ticking from 0 to 142,000+ families with a prominent "100% Open Public Ledger" verification badge.

## Outro / punchline
The interlocking rings brand mark centers with quiet warmth: *"Aim Charity — Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives Together"*, flanked by direct domestic donation options (CBE & Telebirr) and the call to action: *"Stand With Ethiopian Communities"*.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Local Need Verification**: Grassroots elder councils and community volunteers identify vulnerable households directly.
2. **Coalition Logistics Pooling**: Shared warehouses and transport fleets eliminate administrative waste.
3. **Dignified Direct Delivery & Open Ledger**: Relief reaches families directly, followed by publicly published audit reports.
